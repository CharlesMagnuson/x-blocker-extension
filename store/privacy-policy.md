# X Blocker privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

X Blocker does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data or
browsing data.

- The extension runs entirely inside your browser. It has no server, no
  analytics and no account system.
- It inspects the pages you visit only to find and hide Twitter/X links,
  images, icons and embeds. Nothing it finds is recorded or sent anywhere.
- It blocks network requests to x.com, twitter.com, t.co and twimg.com using
  Chrome's built-in declarativeNetRequest rules. The extension itself never
  sees those requests.
- It does not use cookies, local storage or any other persistent storage.

If this ever changes, this policy and the extension's store listing will be
updated before the change ships.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/CharlesMagnuson/x-blocker-extension
